About the Conference

The 32nd Meeting of the Petrology Group of the Mineralogical Society of Poland – PTMin 2027 will be held under the theme “Earth and Beyond: Analytical Frontiers in Geosciences”.

The meeting will focus on the analytical and computational frontiers of modern geoscience – from new microanalytical and imaging techniques to modelling and data-driven approaches. Everyone is welcome: petrologists working on terrestrial rocks and on meteorites, mineralogists and geochemists, as well as researchers studying the environment.

Organised by:

Institute of Geological Sciences,
Polish Academy of Sciences Adam Mickiewicz University

Venue

The conference will take place in the historic 19th-century neo-Gothic palace in Będlewo near Poznań. Set in an extensive romantic park with ponds, the palace houses the Research and Conference Centre of the Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences.
